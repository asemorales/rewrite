> *   **Marketing Innovation:** Implementing a new marketing method involving significant changes in product design or packaging, product placement, product promotion, or pricing. (e.g., Dollar Shave Club's subscription model)
> *   **Organizational Innovation:** Implementing a new organizational method in business practices, workplace organization, or external relations. (e.g., Google's "20% time")
> *   **Business Model Innovation:** Changing the way a business creates, delivers, and captures value. (e.g., Netflix moving from DVDs by mail to streaming)
> *   **Incremental Innovation:** Small-scale improvements to existing products, services, or processes.
